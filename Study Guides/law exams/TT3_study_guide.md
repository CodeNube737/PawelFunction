### Pages 21–30 Summary

- **Right to Participate:**
  - Workers can participate in workplace health and safety activities, such as joining the Joint Occupational Health and Safety Committee (JOHSC).
  - Employers must consult and cooperate with the committee.
  - Consultation is required for exposure control plans and for developing procedures for working alone or in isolation.
  - Similar consultation requirements exist in both provincial and federal regulations.

- **Right to Refuse Unsafe Work:**
  - In BC, refusing unsafe work is a duty if a worker reasonably believes the work could harm themselves or others.
  - Workers must not perform work that poses undue risk and cannot be compelled to do so.
  - The refusal process involves:
    1. Informing the supervisor/employer.
    2. Immediate investigation by the supervisor/employer.
    3. If unresolved, a second investigation with a worker member of the JOHSC or a worker advocate.
    4. If still unresolved, notification of WorkSafeBC (or ESDC) for a third-party investigation.
  - The goal is always to return to safe work.
